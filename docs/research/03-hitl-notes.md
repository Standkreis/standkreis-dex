# 🥾 Step 2 · HITL notes (Sven, 2026-09-04)

**Verdict on the research:** agrees with all of [`01-market-research.md`](01-market-research.md). Open questions from it carry into the grill.

## Seek by iNaturalist (iOS)

| # | Observation | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 😴 **No onboarding.** Lands on a home page, nothing engages. | A dedicated, engaging onboarding is important. |
| 2 | 🎛️ **Global filters are great.** Location + species-group filter shrinks the infinite species list to a personally relevant one. | Filters are first-class, set right away, apply everywhere. |
| 3 | 🧭 Sidebar menu is weird. | Regular **bottom app bar** with the most important pages. |
| 4 | 🏠 Home should focus on **discovery of all species under my current filters**, switchable between **grid, list, map**. | Home = the dex, three views. |
| 5 | 📸 On-camera species recognition **feels amazing**. | Keep live camera ID. |
| 6 | ✋ Camera must not be the only way to add a discovery. | **Claim without proof** allowed, at least for the start. |
| 7 | 📄 Species detail page is great, could be more compact. All info relevant: images, name, Latin name, tags (e.g. *einheimisch*), short description, map, semantics, occurrence (region → global), yearly occurrence, related species. | Keep the full set, tighten layout. |
| 8 | 🌱 **Missing: ecology.** The species' relationship to its environment. Most wanted: **A.** inputs/requirements for the species to occur, **B.** its enemies. Possibly more dimensions not yet known. | Ecology section on detail page; research the dimensions. |
| 9 | 🎯 Missions are a great idea but should be **personal quests / progression**, not a public pool. Being able to accept many missions on day one is overwhelming. | Personal, paced quests. Not crowd-sourcing tasks. |
