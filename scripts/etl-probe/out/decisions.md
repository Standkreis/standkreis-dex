# Grill 0005 · running decisions (source for record 0002)

| # | Decision | Evidence |

| E2 | 🙋 Membership is decided on the **whole year per region**, not per month. Month is a default sort ("jetzt wahrscheinlich") and an opt-in filter "nur jetzt". Cut **per tile**: species that make up **90 %** of the tile's observations, **floor 10** obs, window 2016–2026, observation basis only. Mainz-Bingen → 931 species (🐦 69 🦋 396 🌿 388 🦌 8 🍄 23 🐸 7 🦎 5 ❔ 35). Supersedes record Q2 "per cell and month" and spec "month defaults to now / denominator changes with month" | September lists at ≥5/10/20; seen species vanish out of season; plants/fungi collapse in September; 0.5 %-of-effort rule rejected (scales with effort) |
| E1 | **No grid.** Plausibility is computed on the GADM level-2 polygon (GBIF `gadmGid`), one region = one unit. The 10 km cell survives only on the species map and the location ladder. PLAUSIBILITY loses `cell`, gains `region`. Doubt 13: the Landkreis is the unit, not a label over cells | 10 km union spills +78 % (Mainz, Wiesbaden, Rheingau), 5 of 19 centres inside; polygon call 5–8 s vs 20–35 s per cell |
| E3 | Month = species' **share of all regional observations that month** (effort-normalised), from 12 facet calls per region. Sort "jetzt wahrscheinlich" = share ÷ peak. Chip "nur jetzt" = ≥ 25 % of peak. Year strip: all months ≥ 10 % of peak → "Ganzes Jahr", else runs of months ≥ 25 % of peak. Phenology curves rejected (invent shapes for thin species) | 9/10 test species match; C-Falter Sep 0.10 of peak is the known miss, shown honestly by the bars |
| E4 | **GBIF alone** for the set. iNat research grade is already a GBIF dataset (11 % here); iNat alone misses 40 of GBIF's ≥10 species; needs-ID adds only unvouched IDs. iNat stays for photos (E7) and later export. Refresh = 12 GBIF calls per region, 0 iNat | E4 table: GBIF 217 vs iNat RG 42 species at ≥10 in September |
| E5 | Seven tiles from GBIF ranks: Aves · Mammalia · Amphibia · Squamata+Testudines+Crocodylia · **Insekten & Spinnen = Animalia minus Chordata** (widened: spiders, snails, woodlice, elsewhere crabs) · Plantae · Fungi (lichens included). Fish and other vertebrates outside the four classes stay out until E12 shows a region that needs them. Eighth tile rejected (35 species too thin to pick) | 35 of 931 fit no pure tile: 22 Arachnida, 11 Gastropoda, 2 other arthropods |
| E6 | Species rank only from the GBIF species facet (subspecies already folded). Wikidata QID via P846, fallback exact scientific-name search with rank check, path stored. CoL/ChecklistBank as backbone rejected for now | 874/931 by P846, 55 of the remaining 57 by name; misses include Brennnessel, Margerite, Zunderschwamm |
| E7 | **Image ladder** per species, chosen once in the ETL, attribution stored: 1 iNat taxon default photo if CC0/BY/BY-NC (iNat's square) · 2 Commons P18 unless filename/category says specimen, herbarium, plate, mounted, larva, egg, map (centre crop) · 3 next licensed iNat photo · 4 group icon. Evidence: Commons P18 covers 92 % of the set; 52 % landscape, 27 % portrait, 9 % under 600 px; 19 confirmed specimens or botanical plates by filename, ~25 % flagged loosely. iNat default photos: 100 % present in a 120-species sample, 22 % CC0/BY, 62 % BY-NC, 17 % all rights reserved | assets-run.md, e7-contact-sheet.html |

Kinds dominated by eats/eatenBy, preysOn, hostOf, visitsFlowersOf, and generic interactsWith | coverage-run.md, spot checks |
